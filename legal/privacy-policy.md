# Privacy Policy — Threads of Grace

_Last updated: {{DATE}}_

Threads of Grace ("the app", "we", "us") is a faith-based journaling, prayer, and
Bible-reading app. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. We
designed the app to be **private by default**.

## The short version
- By default, **everything you write stays on your device** (journal entries,
  prayers, bookmarks, mood, settings). We can't read it.
- Cloud features (account, sync, community) are **optional** and only active if
  you create an account.
- We **do not sell your data**, show ads, or use third-party advertising
  trackers.

## What we collect

### When you use the app locally (no account)
- Journal entries, prayers, bookmarks, mood check-ins, reminder time, and
  preferences are stored **only on your device** (local storage). This data is
  not transmitted to us.

### If you create an account (optional, via Supabase)
- **Email address** — to authenticate your account.
- **Synced content** — your journal entries and prayers, so you can access them
  across devices. Stored under row-level security so only you can read them.
- **Community posts** — prayer requests you choose to share are visible to other
  signed-in users. You can post anonymously, and you can delete your posts.
- **Moderation data** — if you report a post, we store that report to keep the
  community safe.

### Device permissions
- **Microphone** — only used when you tap the mic to dictate a journal entry
  (speech-to-text). On Android this uses the device's speech recognition; audio
  is processed to text and is **not stored or sent to us**. You can deny this
  permission and still type normally.
- **Notifications** — used only to deliver the **daily reminder** you set. These
  are scheduled locally on your device.

## How we use data
- To provide journaling, prayer tracking, Bible reading, and (optionally) sync
  and community features.
- To keep the community safe (reporting, automatic hiding of reported content).
- We **do not** use your reflections for advertising or sell them to anyone.

## Data sharing
- Cloud data is processed by **Supabase** (our backend/hosting provider) on our
  behalf. We share data only as needed to operate the service.
- We may disclose data if required by law.

## Data retention & deletion
- Local data is removed when you delete the app or clear app data.
- For accounts, you can delete your content in-app, and request full account
  deletion by contacting us at **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}**.
- You can export your journal at any time from **Settings → Data → Export**.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Scripture
The app includes the **World English Bible** and **King James Version**, both in
the public domain.

We'll update this page and the "last updated" date when this policy changes.
