## Full description (max 4000)
Threads of Grace is a calm, sacred space to reflect, pray, and draw closer to God
— one quiet moment a day.

Beautifully simple and private by design, it brings together everything for a
daily rhythm of faith:

📖 **Read the Word, offline**
The complete Bible is built in — World English Bible and King James Version — so
you can read any chapter, anywhere, with no connection needed. Search a
reference, follow a reading plan, and bookmark verses that speak to you.

✍️ **Journal your reflections**
A gentle, guided flow — choose your mood, receive a fitting verse, and write (or
speak) what's on your heart. Voice journaling lets you reflect hands-free.

🙏 **Pray with purpose**
Keep a living prayer list, mark prayers as answered, and watch your record of
God's faithfulness grow. A guided A.C.T.S. session helps you pray when words are
hard.

🌿 **See your journey**
A mood calendar, gentle insights, and a prayer streak help you notice how God is
meeting you over time.

🤝 **Community (optional)**
Share prayer requests and lift others up on a safe, moderated wall.

🔒 **Private by design**
Your journal lives on your device. We never read, share, or sell your
reflections. Cloud sync and community are entirely optional.

Free to use. No ads. Just you, the Word, and a quiet place to grow.

  their data. **No data sold.** No advertising/analytics SDKs.
